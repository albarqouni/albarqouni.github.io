David and Malek attended the OMI Plenary Meeting in Erlangen 2026, where partners from across the consortium gathered to share progress and align on the road ahead. The meeting hosted work package updates covering the [OMI protocol](https://omi.ikim.nrw/), gateway discussions, AI governance, and the clients. It was interesting to see the application reports from other partner sites, as well as the breakout sessions on implementation and the future of the project beyond its current phase. David and Malek presented our progress report on WP8 the rollout of our AI services.
