Our lab at Universitätsklinikum Bonn, The University of Bonn, and Helmholtz AI in cooperation with with the Lebanese American University, University of Tunis El Manar, and Duhok Polytechnic University are delighted to announce the upcoming BILD Summer School in Tunis, Tunisia between 1 and 5 September 2025. 

BILD 2025 is organized under the umbrella of the [Strategic Arab-German Network for Affordable and Democratized AI in Healthcare (SANAD)](http://localhost:1313/funded/sanad/), uniting academic excellence and technological innovation across borders. Over five intensive days in Tunis, you will explore cutting-edge deep-learning techniques for medical imaging through expert lectures, hands-on labs, and collaborative case studies. Engage with peers and faculty from Germany, Lebanon, Iraq, and Tunisia as you develop practical skills in building and deploying AI models for real-world healthcare challenges. We look forward to an inspiring week of interdisciplinary exchange and the shared commitment to advancing affordable, life-saving AI in medicine.


With participation from   diverse academic backgrounds, we foster   knowledge transfer and   collaborative learning among approximately 50   under-/   graduates, and faculty members from   Germany and  Arab countries.

Apply before 14th July 2024 and join us as we blend  cutting-edge AI with diverse disciplines through   lectures, labs, and a real-world hackathon. Travel tickets, accommodation, and catering will be covered for the whole 5 days of the summer school. 

Further details about the summer school and the application: https://bild2025.github.io/
