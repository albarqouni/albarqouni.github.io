Our lab at Universitätsklinikum Bonn, The University of Bonn, and Helmholtz AI in cooperation with the Arab-German Young Academy of Sciences and Humanities (AGYA) are delighted to announce the upcoming   AGYA   Summer School on   Affordable Artificial Intelligence (SAAI -سعي) in Bonn, 22-26 July 2024. 

The AGYA Summer School is designed to promote theoretical and experimental research in Affordable Artificial Intelligence (AI). Through a carefully curated interdisciplinary   curriculum, participants will explore the   fundamental principles, potential   applications, and the latest advancements in this dynamic scientific field with a multi-faceted focus engaging the academic fields of   health,   education, and   climate change.

With participation from   diverse academic backgrounds, we foster   knowledge transfer and   collaborative learning among approximately 30   under-/   graduates, and 12   faculty members from   Germany and   Arab countries.

Apply before 29th May 2024 and join us as we blend   cutting-edge AI with diverse   disciplines through   lectures, labs, and a real-world   hackathon. Travel tickets, accommodation, and catering will be covered for the whole 5 days of the summer school. 

Further details about the summer school and the application: https://saai2024.github.io/
