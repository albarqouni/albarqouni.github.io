## SANAD Symposium on AI in Medicine & Food Sciences

Over the past two days, we had the pleasure of hosting our **SANAD symposium on AI in Medicine and Food Sciences** within the framework of the **DAAD Ta’ziz project**, welcoming a delegation from **Tunisia**, **Lebanon**, and **Iraq**.  
Project link: https://albarqouni.github.io/funded/symposium/

The symposium brought together colleagues from **Universitätsklinikum Bonn**, **The University of Bonn**, and our partner universities in the MENA region: **Lebanese American University**, **University of Tunis El Manar**, and **Duhok Polytechnic University**. Together, we focused on strengthening **academic cooperation**, **interdisciplinary exchange**, and **capacity building** under the **SANAD** vision.

We extend our gratitude to **Prof. Björn Krüger** for his excellent keynote, to all speakers and participants for their contributions, and to our dedicated team for their support.

Special thanks to **Dr. Ismail Acir** and his team for co-organizing and hosting the **Food Sciences Day** with great hospitality.

Beyond the academic program, our delegation enjoyed a rich cultural experience — from the **Christmas market in Bonn** to the **Chocolate Museum** and the **Cologne Cathedral**.

We look forward to building on these connections and continuing our collaboration.
