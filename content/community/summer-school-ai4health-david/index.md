David attended  the AI4Health Summer School 2023, organized collaboratively by the French Health Data Hub and French AI research institutions at PariSanté Campus, was an incredible experience. The program focused on advanced methods in medical AI, covering critical aspects of deep learning for medical imaging. I actively participated in two workshops:

- Deep Learning for Medical Image Processing: Exploring innovative techniques for tumor segmentation and detection in whole-body 3D MRIs, focusing on patch-based approaches, denoising, contrast correction, and augmentations.
- Fed-BioMed Workshop: Examining federated learning approaches tailored for biomedical applications, addressing challenges in privacy-preserving data sharing across institutions.

The event was a truly remarkable opportunity to delve deeper into these cutting-edge topics and engage with peers and leaders in medical AI.
