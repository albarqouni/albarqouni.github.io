We are delighted to share that two members of our group have received awards supporting their participation in [MICCAI 2026](https://conferences.miccai.org/2026/en/default.asp), the 29th International Conference on Medical Image Computing and Computer Assisted Intervention, taking place from September 27 to October 1, 2026, in Strasbourg, France.

**David** has been selected to receive the **2026 MICCAI Society Registration Grant**, awarded by the MICCAI Society through its [Registration and Travel Grants](https://miccai.org/conference-awards/miccai-registration-and-travel-grants/) program. The grant is awarded to students and early-career scientists who are authors of an accepted MICCAI 2026 paper and covers the in-person main conference registration, both days of satellite event registrations, and one year of MICCAI Society membership. The MICCAI Society awards approximately 25 Registration Grants per year across the whole community.

**Adea** has been awarded funding under the University of Bonn's [Participation in International Conferences](https://www.uni-bonn.de/en/research-and-teaching/ecrs/during-your-doctorate/support/participation-international-conferences) program for doctoral researchers, administered by the Bonn Graduate Center. The award covers travel, accommodation, and conference participation fees for doctoral students actively presenting their work at an international conference.

Both awards will be used to attend MICCAI 2026 in Strasbourg, where they will present our accepted paper **"PromptGate: Client-Adaptive Vision–Language Gating for Open-Set Federated Active Learning"**. MICCAI 2026 received a record number of submissions this year: [1,166 papers were accepted, corresponding to an acceptance rate of around 26%](https://miccai-explorer.github.io/year/2026.html).

We thank the MICCAI Society and the University of Bonn for their support, and we look forward to presenting our work and meeting the community in Strasbourg.
