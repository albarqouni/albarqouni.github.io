I was fortunate enough to attend and participate at the DAAD PRIME Alumni Seminar where I had the chance to meet and re-connect with PRIME fellows and a couple of colleagues! Although I received the [PRIME fellowship](https://www.daad.de/en/studying-in-germany/scholarships/daad-funding-programmes/prime/prime-fellows-201819/) 6 years ago, I felt it was just yesterday! Gratfeul to the DAAD for this fellowship and tremendous efforts to reconnect with our fellows!
