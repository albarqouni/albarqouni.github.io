Elodie, David, and Shadi participated at the Helmholtz ELLIS Workshop on Foundation Models which took place in Berlin over two days, bringing together leading researchers and practitioners from academia and industry. The primary focus was to explore the transformative impact of large-scale AI models (\textit{Foundation Models}) across various scientific domains, including medical imaging, astrophysics, tabular data analysis, and beyond.
