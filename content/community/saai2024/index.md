We have just wrapped up an amazing week at the [Summer School on Affordbale AI (SAAI -- سعي)](https://saai2024.github.io/).

As the curtains close on an unforgettable week in Bonn, we reflect on the enriching experiences shared at the AGYA Summer School on Affordable AI. We were thrilled to host 60 guests, including 35 students from nations across the globe; 🇲🇷 Mauritania, 🇲🇦 Morocco, 🇹🇳 Tunisia, 🇩🇿 Algeria, 🇱🇾 Libya, 🇪🇬 Egypt, 🇵🇸 Palestine, 🇱🇧 Lebanon, 🇯🇴 Jordan, 🇸🇾 Syria, 🇮🇶 Iraq, 🇴🇲 Oman, 🇶🇦 Qatar, 🇰🇼 Kuwait, 🇸🇦Saudi, 🇦🇪 UAE, 🇾🇪 Yemen, 🇰🇲 Comoros, 🇩🇪 Germany, 🇷🇺 Russia, 🇮🇹 Italy, 🇺🇦 Ukraine, 🇫🇷 France, and 🇨🇴 Colombia alongside 15 faculty and AGYA members, and 6 dedicated tutors.

🚀 Our diverse participants engaged deeply with innovative AI concepts, learning from esteemed speakers, lecturers, and each other. The collaborative environment fostered not only education but also meaningful connections across cultural lines.

🌐 Social events at iconic locations like the Cologne Cathedral, Drachenburg Castle, and the Haus der Geschichte Museum provided fantastic backdrops for our participants to unwind and network, enhancing their cultural and academic experience.

Thanks to everyone who made this event a huge success—your enthusiasm and insights have truly shaped this summer school into a beacon of learning and international cooperation.
 
Special Thanks to the Arab-German Young Academy of Sciences and Humanities (AGYA) for the financial support. I would like to thank The University of Bonn, Universitätsklinikum Bonn, Helmholtz AI for the host and financial support as well.
