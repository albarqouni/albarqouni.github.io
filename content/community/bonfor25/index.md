At the 23rd BONFOR Symposium on Wednesday, 7 May 2025, held in the DZNE Lecture Hall at University Hospital Bonn, Albarqouni Lab actively showcased its current research by delivering concise oral presentations during the afternoon’s Doctoral Promotion Fair. Our team highlighted a diverse portfolio of projects—ranging from advanced image‐analysis pipelines to AI-driven diagnostic tools—and outlined potential MD-thesis topics for interested students, fostering in-depth discussions about methodological approaches and future research directions. By engaging directly with medical students, the lab not only raised awareness of its work but also provided guidance on how to embark on cutting-edge thesis projects within our group, thereby strengthening the pipeline of young clinician-scientists
