Despite the difficult circumstances, we are proud to contribute to MICCAI 2024 with the following papers and events:

- Our paper on Affordable DL for common and inherited retinal diseases will be presented at the 11th Ophthalmology Image Analysis (OMIA) Workshop on 10th October, AM Session, Cristal Room - Palmeraie Palace. Further details can be found here: http://hdmilab.cn/omia11. 

with Elyes Farjallah, Razieh Rezaei, Philipp Herrmann, Sandrine Kuenzel, Frank G. Holz from Universitätsklinikum Bonn, and Said El Shamieh from Beirut Arab University. 
 
- Our paper on Leveraging Planning annotations in Adaptive Radiation Therapy will be presented at #CLIP Workshop on 6th October, AM Session, Conference Center.  Further details can be found here: https://miccai-clip.org/. 

with Francesca De Benetti, Yousef Yaganeh, Claus Belka, Stefanie Corradini, Nassir Navab, Christopher Kurz, Guillaume Landry, and Thomas Wendler

Other exciting events during the conference include:
- #RISE Networking Event where Nassir Navab will deliver an inspiring talk on the democratization of healthcare (https://lnkd.in/dM6y89JU). 
- Our 5th workshop on Distributed, Collaborative, and Federated Learning (#DeCaF) -- will be held on 10th October, PM Session (https://lnkd.in/ex5sQ_ga).

On a personal note, serving as Challenges Co-Chair at #MICCAI2024 has been incredibly difficult, especially with my family being under threat in #Gaza during these troubling times. However, I’ve pushed through, believing that #resilience in the face of adversity will make us stronger.

Looking ahead, I’m optimistic about the future and excited for a productive year filled with new achievements. I’m especially looking forward to meeting and interacting with #young #talents at #MICCAI in #Marrakech next week.
