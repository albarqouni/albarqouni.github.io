The lab is thrilled to contribute to the organization of [MICCAI'25](https://conferences.miccai.org/2025/en/default.asp) in Daejon, South Korea. This conference is particularly relevant to the lab's research focus, and we are honored to be part of this journey to advance innovation in AI in Healthcare! 

Prof. Dr. Shadi Albarqouni is serving on the Outreach Committee, working to foster collaboration and engagement with the community. Dr. Elodie Germani will serve as an Area Chair, participating in managing the scientific review process and ensuring the quality of accepted papers.
