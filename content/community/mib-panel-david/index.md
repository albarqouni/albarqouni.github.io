It was an exciting to present our research on Lymph Node Metastasis Detection in *Pancreatic Ductal Adenocarcinoma* at the MIB Future Panel 2024. Hosted by the *Medical Imaging Center Bonn* in partnership with *Universitätsklinikum Bonn* and the Medical Faculty of *Rheinische Friedrich-Wilhelms-Universität Bonn*, this symposium united thought leaders from both academia and industry. The event highlighted the critical importance of multi-center collaborations for the development of robust Computer-Aided Diagnosis (CAD) systems, which are essential for providing radiologists with reliable tools suited for real-world clinical application.
