David represented our group at the BONFOR Symposium 2026 in Bonn, taking part in the Promotionsbörse (doctoral thesis fair) organized by the Medical Student Council alongside the symposium at the BMZ I. We have presented the work of the Computational Medical Imaging Research group (Albarqouni AG) at the Clinic for Diagnostic and Interventional Radiology and shared what doing a doctoral thesis with us looks like. It is a great opportunity to connect with the medical students looking for a thesis topic, walk them through our research directions in medical imaging and machine learning, and address their questions about getting started in research.
