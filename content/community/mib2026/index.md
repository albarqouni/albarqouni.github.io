[Malek](https://albarqouni.github.io/authors/malek-al-abed/) attended MIB Future Panel 2026 where he also pitched [MRIQT-Neo](http://albarqouni.github.io/mriqt), along with [Anne](https://albarqouni.github.io/authors/anne-groteklaes/), at the Medical Pitch Contest and our plan to commercialze the project. The MIB Future Panel is UKBonn's annual networking event hosted by the Medical Imaging Center and UKBonn's Transfer Team in Bonn. The goal of this event is to merge medical imaging and artificial intelligence (AI) in clinical practice and research with a panel discussion with specilists. The bulk of the event features a medical pitch contest featuring teams from the medical faculty at the University Hospital Bonn, where venture capital investors serve as a jury, evaluating the competing teams projects for a competing prize of €60,000. The event offers an opportunity for teams to network with investors and accelerator programs to move from the research phase into commercialization.
Our work, MRIQT-Neo is a software that works on image quality transfer for MRI images, from the portable ultra-low-field MRI into high-quality, 3T- like images.
