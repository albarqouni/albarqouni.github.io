**Placeholder — the interactive demo is in preparation.**

Colour variation between labs and slide scanners is one of the main reasons histology models fail on new sites. This demo will let you upload an H&E image tile and transfer it to a reference stain style, comparing a learned approach ([StainGAN](/publications/shaban-2019-staingan/)) with classical stain-normalisation methods. See also the [stain-normalization code](/code/stain-normalization/).
