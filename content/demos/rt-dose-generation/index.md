**Placeholder — the interactive demo is in preparation.**

Online adaptive radiotherapy re-plans treatment while the patient is on the table, which requires dose distributions within seconds. This demo will generate a dose distribution from the planning image and contoured structures, developed within the DFG project [MRI-Guided Online Adaptive Radiotherapy](/projects/radiotherapy/) with LMU and TU Munich.
