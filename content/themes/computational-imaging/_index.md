We develop the core methods of **computational medical imaging**: medical foundation and vision-language models,
multimodal learning that fuses imaging with clinical data, and principled **uncertainty** estimation so that models know
when they do not know. Our work spans radiology (MRI, CT, X-ray, ultrasound, mammography), histopathology,
ophthalmology and endoscopy.

**Key questions**
- How do we build foundation models that transfer across organs, modalities and tasks with little labelled data?
- How can imaging, text and clinical data be combined into one coherent model?
- How do we quantify and communicate uncertainty to clinicians?
