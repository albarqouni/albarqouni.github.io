Clinical data are scattered across hospitals and cannot simply be pooled. With **federated learning** we train and
evaluate models across institutions while the data stay where they are — handling heterogeneous (non-IID) data,
label scarcity, personalisation and fairness between sites. Through OMI and the Medical Informatics Initiative we bring
these methods to remote AI inference across German university hospitals.

**Key questions**
- How can hospitals train a shared model when their data are non-IID and cannot leave the premises?
- How do we personalise a federated model for each site while keeping it fair to all?
- How do we generate multi-centre clinical evidence without moving patient data?
