Most medical AI is built for, and with data from, well-resourced hospitals. We work on **affordable and responsible AI**:
models that are fair and robust across populations, scanners and resource settings; image-quality transfer for portable
and ultra-low-field devices; lightweight and explainable models; and guidelines for trustworthy AI in healthcare
(FUTURE-AI). Capacity building — summer schools, symposia and the SANAD network — is part of this theme.

**Key questions**
- Does a model work equally well for every patient group and every hospital?
- Can AI close the quality gap between low-cost and high-end imaging devices?
- How do we grow sustainable AI communities in the Global South?
