This collaboration explored federated learning for musculoskeletal segmentation in CT imaging across institutions in Germany and Japan.
Using labeled CT data from NAIST and an unlabeled whole-body CT cohort from University Hospital Bonn, we evaluated cross-site generalization and domain transfer under realistic scanner and population shifts.
In parallel, a secure federated learning infrastructure was deployed and ethical approval obtained at University Hospital Bonn. While the project did not progress to publication-ready federated training due to annotation constraints and deployment complexity, it provided valuable methodological and organizational insights.

### Perspective

The collaboration strengthened institutional expertise in federated learning under real-world clinical constraints and informed subsequent national and international projects with more harmonized data availability.
