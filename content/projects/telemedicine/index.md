Access to specialist care in Palestine is limited, which makes telemedicine a natural way to bring expertise to patients rather than patients to expertise.

### What we did

- **2008 — a self-initiated plan for a telemedicine centre.** Laid out the steps towards establishing a telemedicine centre in Palestine ([EHB 2009](/publications/albarqouni-2009-steps/)).
- **2014 — consultancy for GIZ.** Advised the Deutsche Gesellschaft für Internationale Zusammenarbeit (GIZ) on telemedicine in Palestine.
