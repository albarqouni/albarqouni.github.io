This joint teaching activity was developed within a BMFTR-funded international program to strengthen academic exchange and capacity building in artificial intelligence for medical imaging.
The course combines lectures, hands-on exercises, and project-based learning, enabling students to work on clinically motivated imaging problems using AI methods.

### Perspective

The initiative contributes to sustainable academic exchange between Germany and Egypt and provides a basis for continued joint teaching activities, student exchange, and integration of research-led topics into international curricula.

Selected Interdisciplinary Research with Methodological AI Contributions

In addition to my core research in artificial intelligence for medical imaging and clinical applications, I have contributed to a small number of exploratory interdisciplinary projects in materials science and food science. These activities focused on transferring methodological expertise in data-driven modeling and machine learning to neighboring domains and align with the University of Bonn’s Transdisciplinary Research Areas *Sustainable Futures*, *Modelling*, and *Life and Health*. Together, they demonstrate the transferability of AI methodologies and my engagement in transdisciplinary research initiatives without detracting from my primary research focus.
