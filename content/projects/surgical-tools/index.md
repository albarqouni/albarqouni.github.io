This exploratory collaboration investigated state-space vision models for robust surgical tool segmentation under realistic visual corruptions.
In collaboration with Kyung Hee University, we participated in the MICCAI EndoVis 2024 SegSTRONG-C challenge, benchmarking robustness to non-adversarial corruptions such as smoke, bleeding, and illumination changes.
The proposed Vision Mamba–UNet approach achieved competitive performance across corruption types and ranked 7th overall among international submissions.

### Perspective

The project supported early exploration of state-space models in medical image analysis and international doctoral mentorship. A jointly submitted DAAD exchange proposal, although not funded, laid the groundwork for continued collaboration and future joint research activities.
