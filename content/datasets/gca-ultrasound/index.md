Curated with the Center for Rare Rheumatological Diseases (Claus-Jürgen Bauer, Prof. Valentin Schäfer), University Hospital Bonn. One of the largest clinically validated datasets for ultrasound-based GCA diagnosis; being extended towards a multi-center German cohort.

Our lab led the ML-readiness: quality control, cleaning, standardised preprocessing, annotation workflow and training/validation splits.
